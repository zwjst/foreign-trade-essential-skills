# Handoff, statuses, and CRM continuity

## Lead statuses
New; research in progress; identity unresolved; identity confirmed; contact research in progress; contact route identified; first touch drafted; first touch sent; awaiting reply; auto-reply only; human reply; clinical need confirmed; no current need; referred; technical evaluation; quotation; negotiation; won; lost; nurture; paused; do not contact.

## Contact statuses
Not searched; public source found; ownership uncertain; owner confirmed; not tested; auto-reply; human response; invalid; opted out.

## Message log
For each event store timestamp/time zone, channel, sender, recipient type, message summary, actual sent status, response type, source transcript, next action, and consent/opt-out status.

## Handoff to client-progress-summary
Pass only facts supported by the supplied conversation:
- Country/city/specialty or verified role
- Whether company/product introduction actually happened
- Actual topics discussed
- Actual customer feedback
- Evidence-based demand fit
- What was actually asked and whether a reply arrived
- Next action, clearly marked as planned or completed

Never pass raw personal contact details into a public example or CRM paragraph unless explicitly required by the user's CRM schema.
