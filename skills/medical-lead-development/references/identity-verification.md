# Identity verification reference

## Objective
Establish whether records refer to the same professional and whether the listed practice is current.

## Search matrix
Search exact full name, name variants, specialty, city, institution, and professional title. Use local language equivalents. For Spanish names, preserve both surnames and test punctuation/diacritic variants without collapsing identities.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Government professional registration / provider registry
2. Official institution biography or staff directory
3. Professional association / specialty society
4. University / official academic profile
5. Journal publisher page / DOI author metadata
6. Conference organizer or speaker page
7. Official clinic profile / appointment portal
8. Reputable news/interview
9. General directories and social profiles

## Currentness
Record publication/update dates. An old conference biography or archived hospital page may establish a past affiliation, not a current one. A business registry may establish a legal entity, not current clinical activity or ownership.

## Conflict handling
Do not average contradictions away. Show each version, dates, and source quality. Keep records separate until resolved.
