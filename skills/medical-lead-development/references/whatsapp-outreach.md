# WhatsApp outreach reference

## Choose the correct recipient
Doctor-direct, clinic, assistant, department, hospital switchboard, or third party. Address only the recipient actually reached.

## First message: identity confirmation
Spanish:
“Hola, Dr./Dra. [Nombre]. Buenos días. Disculpe que le contacte. Quisiera confirmar si estoy comunicándome directamente con [Nombre completo], especialista en [especialidad]. Muchas gracias por su ayuda.”

English:
“Hello Dr. [Name], good morning. Sorry to contact you unexpectedly. May I confirm whether I am speaking directly with [Full Name], the [specialty] physician? Thank you for your time.”

Clinic / assistant:
“Hola, buenos días. Quisiera confirmar si el Dr./la Dra. [Nombre] actualmente atiende en su institución. ¿Sería posible indicarme el canal adecuado para hacerle llegar una consulta profesional?”

Always include Chinese translations for Spanish/English drafts.

## After confirmation
Introduce sender and manufacturer accurately. Ask one relevant clinical question. Keep it concise and natural.

## Do not
- Send catalogue before confirming identity or relevance.
- Claim the number is personal without evidence.
- Send multiple questions in one message.
- Re-send content already sent.
- Treat automated reply as doctor confirmation.
- Continue after refusal / opt-out.
