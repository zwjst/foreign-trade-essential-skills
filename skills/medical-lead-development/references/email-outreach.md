# Email outreach reference

## Recipient classification
Named professional email; institution email; department email; general contact; appointment inbox; unknown.

## First-touch email
If recipient identity is unconfirmed, use a concise professional identity/routing confirmation. For a generic inbox, ask the institution to route the message rather than implying the inbox belongs to the doctor.

## After prior WhatsApp contact
Use email for complementary information, requested technical documents, product application notes, or formal follow-up. Do not duplicate the WhatsApp opening verbatim.

## Email structure
- Specific subject
- Appropriate greeting
- One sentence establishing sender/company identity
- Evidence-based reason for contacting this recipient
- One clear question or request
- Professional close

If an automatic signature is configured, do not add a duplicate name or company signature. Do not state attachments were included unless they actually are. Provide Chinese translation for foreign-language drafts.
