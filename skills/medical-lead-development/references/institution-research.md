# Institution and procurement research reference

## Institution record
- Official name and aliases
- Country / city / public institutional address
- Legal status and ownership type
- Facility type
- Departments and services
- Branches / affiliations
- Clinical and technical contacts
- Procurement process
- Existing equipment and source dates
- Expansion / new-room signals
- Distributor or service partner
- Open questions

## Procurement source types
- Government procurement portals
- Tender notices and specifications
- Award / contract notices
- Public budget and annual purchasing plans
- Hospital board / annual reports
- Official press releases
- Installation and commissioning announcements
- Public supplier case studies (corroborate)
- Import / regulatory public records where lawful and accessible

## Evidence labels
Current installed; historical installed; tendered; awarded; planned; competitor claim; unverified mention.

## Commercial comparison
Always align:
- Currency and date
- Incoterm
- Freight and insurance
- Duties and taxes
- Installation and training
- Warranty and service
- Accessories and workstation
- Regulatory/registration costs
Do not compare FOB against door-to-door prices as equivalent.
