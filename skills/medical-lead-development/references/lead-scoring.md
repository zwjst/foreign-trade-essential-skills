# Lead scoring reference

## Weights
| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Clinical application fit | 25 |
| Purchase / replacement likelihood | 20 |
| Institution commercial value | 15 |
| Decision influence / buyer access | 15 |
| Contact quality / reachability | 10 |
| Channel / partnership value | 10 |
| Information completeness | 5 |
| Total | 100 |

## Scoring anchors
For each dimension, score only the evidence-supported portion:
- 0% of weight: no evidence or clearly unsuitable.
- 25%: weak / indirect / old evidence.
- 50%: plausible but not validated.
- 75%: strong and current evidence.
- 100%: directly confirmed and commercially actionable.

Do not use this as a mechanical rule when the dimension has a different natural scale; explain any score.

## Grades
- A 80–100: priority development
- B 60–79: qualified development
- C 40–59: nurture / further qualification
- D 0–39: low priority / pause

## Confidence safeguards
Show evidence confidence separately from numeric score. Identity conflicts prevent A classification. Specialty inference alone cannot justify a high clinical-fit score. No credible route prevents full contact score. A doctor can be an influencer without being the buyer.
