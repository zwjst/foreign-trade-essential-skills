# Clinical and specialty analysis reference

## Evidence labels
- Publicly documented: official clinical service page, biography, procedure page, or reliable publication.
- Self-reported: prospect states it in a conversation supplied by the user.
- Institution-published: the institution states the service is available; does not prove the doctor personally performs it.
- Specialty inference: plausible based on specialty but not verified.
- Unknown: no reliable evidence.

## Analysis template
1. Broad specialty
2. Subspecialty
3. Actual procedures
4. Imaging modalities used
5. Intraoperative / image-guided use
6. Current equipment (if public)
7. Unanswered clinical question

## Avoid common errors
- Specialty does not equal procedure list.
- Procedure does not automatically require X-ray or fluoroscopy.
- A surgeon not using C-arm does not prove the hospital lacks one.
- A hospital offering imaging does not prove the doctor controls the department.
- Do not equate a mobile C-arm with a fixed cath lab.
- Distinguish radiography, fluoroscopy, angiography, ultrasound, CT, MRI, and CBCT.
