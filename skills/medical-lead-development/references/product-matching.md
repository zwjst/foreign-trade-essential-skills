# Perlove product matching reference

## Match from use case, not specialty keyword
| Product family | Potential use case | Required validation |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile DR | ICU, emergency, bedside | Bed count, workflow, detector, mobility, power |
| Fixed DR | General radiography | Room, workload, detector, generator, local power |
| Suspended DR | High-throughput radiography | Room dimensions, ceiling structure, movement |
| Dynamic DR / DRF | Fluoroscopy plus radiography | Actual dynamic exams, pulse/frame needs |
| Mobile C-arm | Intraoperative fluoroscopy | Procedure types, anatomy, dose, detector, mobility |
| Interventional C-arm | Selected interventions | Clinical scope, image quality, dose, regulatory indication |
| 3D C-arm | Intraoperative 3D imaging | 3D workflow, image volume, OR geometry |
| CBCT | Dental/maxillofacial/selected applications | FOV, voxel, local approved indications |

## Technical validation checklist
Generator output; kV/mA/mAs; pulse/frame rate; tube model and heat capacity; focal spots; detector size/pixel pitch/resolution; mechanical travel; SID; power phase/voltage/frequency; line impedance; DICOM/PACS; workstation; printer; accessories; lead protection; installation; training; warranty; service; regulatory registration.

Never transfer a specification from one model to another. Confirm exact model suffix and market version from current official documentation.
